# Owner and legal approval checklist — DRAFT, not a finalized legal policy

The in-app templates in src/policies.ts are visibly marked drafts. New signup acknowledges reading draft notices; this is not represented as approval of final legally compliant terms. Complete the items below with the actual owner/adviser. No business identity, address, jurisdiction, retention term or support mailbox has been invented.

- Legal controller/operator identity, business/trading name, postal address and applicable contact details.
- Official support route: set SUPPORT_URL to an approved HTTPS page or mailto address; test it. The current fallback tells users to contact the person who supplied the installation and retain their request reference. That fallback is appropriate only for controlled testing.
- Hosting/database/email provider names, locations, subprocessors, international transfers and relevant agreements.
- Applicable markets, legal bases, privacy rights, age/parental eligibility and child-related data minimization obligations.
- Governing law, dispute resolution, acceptable use, service limits, disclaimers and consumer rights reviewed by counsel.
- Data categories and shared visibility, including the retained financial ledger, free-text personal data, pseudonymous attribution IDs and deletion audit records.
- Exact retention periods and grounds for account/action-token/security logs/shared financial records/deletion audit/backup copies; legal-hold decision owner and release process.
- Deletion review SLA, verified identity checks, support escalation, backup expiration, private downloads/browser caches and how completed deletions are replayed after restore.
- Define operator authorization for the completion command and a private external deletion register; rehearse member and owner cases. Archival is not ownership transfer or erasure of shared history.
- Account recovery and verification sender identity, provider delivery/spam handling, support for undeliverable addresses and suspected compromise.
- Google Fonts availability/privacy: verify the existing external font resource or review a separately approved self-hosting change.
- Accessibility review, support/incident ownership, breach response and host monitoring/backup alert destinations.
- If selling access, finalize pricing, entitlements, cancellation/refund/support commitments and commercial terms. No billing/access-entitlement system was added in Phase 2.
- Replace draft product copy with owner/legal-approved text; update frontend/backend policy version together, and decide acknowledgment requirements for existing accounts. Do not merely remove the word Draft.
- Record owner/adviser approval, version, date and the actual release gates before real public collection.

The application does not accept bank credentials or process payments. Reimbursement entries represent money received elsewhere. Potential offsets are informational and do not create recorded payments. Never promise complete deletion where shared history, approved legal holds, backups or user-controlled copies remain.
